FORTUNAFARMS

Castle Rock, Colorado

Built for the horses who live here

Fortuna Farms is a small private hunter/jumper facility just a couple miles west of I-25 at the base of the Rocky Mountains.

The stable has 8 oversized stalls (16' x 14') with automatic hay feeders and waterers as well as attached 32' runs.

The 100' x 200' indoor arena is warm in the winter, but cool and shaded in the summer with 12 windows. The dust-free footing and lots of natural light make it ideal for training. Although the horses agree, it’s the 5 irrigated summer grass pastures and the 3 mud-free winter turnouts for maximum turnout that they love the most.
